🎯 2L-T Active Hunt Playbook

The scanner watches everything that's listed. Most 2L-Ts on earth aren't — they sit in wrecking yards, exporter warehouses, and driveways. These steps make that hidden inventory come to you. Total effort: ~30 minutes, once.

1. Post the wanted ad (copy & paste)

WTB: Toyota 2L-T turbo diesel engine (mechanical injection pump) — complete engine, running or rebuildable core. Also interested in a 2L-TE with manual transmission, or a complete diesel donor truck (Hilux / Surf / LN106 / LN130). Cash ready, can arrange freight from anywhere. david@justice.engineering
  • ih8mud → Classifieds → "Parts Wanted" — the #1 place a stored engine surfaces
  • Craigslist "wanted" in Seattle, Portland, Sacramento, Denver — diesel country; wanted ads get replies listings never would
  • Facebook groups: Toyota Diesel Owners, Toyota Diesel Trucks/Swaps, JDM Engine Classifieds — and set the Marketplace saved-search alert while you're there

2. Blast the wrecker networks (one form → hundreds of yards)

  • 🇦🇺 Partslocator (Australia) — diesel Hiluxes get dismantled weekly; yards email you offers
  • 🇬🇧 BreakerYard and 1st Choice Spares (UK) — every UK Surf import ends up in this network
  • 🇳🇿 Need A Part (NZ) — NZ is Surf country
  • 🇺🇸 car-part.com — search "Toyota Pickup, Engine Assembly, 1984–86" — US salvage-yard inventory network

Request wording for all of them: "Toyota 2L-T 2.4 turbo diesel complete engine — Hilux/Surf/Land Cruiser LJ70. Will ship internationally."

3. Call the specialists (they source on request)

  • Diesel Toys (TX) — sources 2L-T/2L-TE through their Japan pipeline for conversion builds; ask to be on the list
  • Torfab (Everett, WA) — Land Cruiser diesel specialists, close to you
  • JDM Engine Depot (NJ) — ask them to flag a 2L-T on an upcoming container

4. If nothing bites: widen the target

  • 2L-TE + pump swap — same long block; swapping to a mechanical 2L-T pump is a known conversion. The board usually holds several 2L-TE + manual-trans units.
  • 3L (2.8 NA) + Toyota's own turbo kit — the community's availability-rich route; genuine kits show on eBay AU.
  • Buy the whole donor truck — often cheaper than the engine alone; every bracket included.
